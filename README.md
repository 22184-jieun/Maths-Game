# Maths-Game
This is a maths game for AS91906, AS91907

**This program uses Pygame-ce (Pygame Community Edition). Please install the module before running the code, as IT WILL NOT function otherwise.**
**(Note that it _must_ be pygame-ce and _not_ just pygame)**

Please install the fonts provided in the Assets folder prior to running the code.
Additionally, as this program contains music, please ensure volume is set to an appropriate level prior to running the program.

Instructions & Troubleshooting

Menu Page:
- Press the 'START' button to proceed to the character selection page.
- Press the 'LEADERBOARD' button to view the leaderboard page.
- Press the 'QUIT' button to end to program.
- Press the '?' to turn the music on / off.

Leaderboard Page:
- Press the heart shaped buttons to select the corressponding difficulty level scoreboard to search.
- Press the 'v' button to open the drop-down selection box. Scroll to view more buttons. Select a maths concept from the avaiable options. Click and drag to scroll is not possible.
- Difficulty and maths concepts must be chosen before pressing the 'Confirm' button to search for records in that specification.
- Press the 'B' button to return to the menu.

Character Selection Page:
- Press any of the three 'Select?' buttons to choose the character in the corresponding icon above.
- Click on the box with the text ' E.g. John' to enter your name.
- Once the above steps are completed, press the 'Confirm' button to proceed to the maths concept selection page.
- Press the 'B' button to return to the menu.

Maths Concept Selection Page:
- Press any of the 6 large buttons to choose the maths concept specified on the button.
- Press the 'Confirm' button to proceed into the difficulty selection page.
- Press the 'B' button to return to the menu.

Difficulty Selection Page:
- Press any of the 'easy', 'medium', and 'hard' buttons to select your difficulty.
- Press the 'Play' button to begin the game.
- Press the 'B' button to return to the menu.

GAMEPLAY:
- The first phase of the gameplay features a timer of 1 minute and 10 questions. The player 'stacks' pancakes by answering questinos. The timing of the answers does not matter. The player must answer 10 questions within the time limit. The player will not be penalised for not being able to do so.
- When the player answers 10 questions correctly or the time runs out, they recieve bonus points corresponding to the amount of questions they answered correctly.
- The second part of the gameplay is the 'decorating phase'. Each question the player answers correctly they gain 1 'action point' which they can use to place one topping / sauce on the pancake.
  To do this the player can click on one the the 'sauce bottles' or 'trays' that appear on the right side of the screen, then click on the 'pancake' to place them.
  The player must fufil the random 'order' / 'receipt' which is the illustration on the top left corner of the screen.
  Once the player fufils the requirements, the player is awarded points and the gameplay loops.
